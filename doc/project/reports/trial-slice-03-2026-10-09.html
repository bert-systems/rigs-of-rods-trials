Where the work now happens

The timer starts at pilot BeginStep and ends after native phases, job barriers, enabled energy reduction and aggregate queue copy. Probe sampling and queueing follow the timer in both modes. Separate writer threads perform I/O; scheduler/contention effects can still affect elapsed time. These are wall-step durations, not process CPU time or a bare upstream benchmark.

Optimizations preserve native force-call order and arithmetic: sparse touched-channel clearing/reduction, skipping observation for audited inactive phases, carrying the previous phase boundary forward in Delta, bypassing empty inter-actor/free-force observations, avoiding unused beam geometry scans and table-driven IEEE CRC-32 in writer threads.
