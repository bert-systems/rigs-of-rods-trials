The prototype exposed a feedback loop: reset-before-sync made a slow flush immediately trigger another flush. The repair reduced 70 synchronization calls to 10 on these individual runs. Final D: wall time was 60.2 s, versus 91.3 s for the instrumented prototype and 52.9 s for the original baseline. Thus this evidence does not establish a general end-to-end speedup over the original build. Disk variability, shared I/O, and one run per detail variant limit that claim. The final C: barrier took 24.1 s on a different volume; it is not a matched-drive algorithm comparison.

One final D: synchronization blocked for 16.60 s. Peak queue occupancy was 13,734 / 15,709 frames (87.4%). The separate ~3 GiB queue and 820,429,056-byte history reservation remain necessary. Producer copy traffic fell from 4,921,344,000 to 3,102,745,920 bytes; raw window payload remained 1,550,318,264 bytes. No rates, required windows, coverage thresholds or resource preflight floors were reduced.
