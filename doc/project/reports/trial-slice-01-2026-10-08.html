Complete capture is separate from scientific acceptance.

Actual simulation and workbench

Recorded quantities and their interpretation

For the movable-node cohort, the observer records mass-weighted center, momentum, kinetic energy, total force actually consumed at the velocity update and the added ground/object-contact force. It measures kinetic work using midpoint velocity at that same update boundary. World axes are native Y-up; units are SI.

p = Σ m v                         K = ½ Σ m |v|²
rₚ = Σ [m(v_after − v_before) − F_consumed Δt]
W_update = Σ F_consumed · ½(v_before + v_after) Δt
rₖ = Σ [K_after − K_before − W_update]

The observed mass is 11156.339 kg. The primary run's maximum momentum-update residual is 0.00068171213 kg·m/s and maximum kinetic-work-update residual is 0.0032201812 J. These small residuals confirm consistency at the numerical update boundary; they do not establish closed energy accounting across springs, damping, friction, plasticity, breaking, propulsion or fluid/aerodynamic models.

These plots use recorded native summaries, reduced only for rendering. Release is a marked initialization boundary, with body/wheel velocity set coherently after settling. The archive remains authoritative for every recorded tick. Live chart samples are a reduced projection and do not preserve every component peak.

Environment behavior

Trials configure negative scalar gravity, explicit air density and a constant world-space wind. The pilot's existing generic drag path uses air-relative velocity and density scaling; its legacy stochastic draws are retained. Dry-air temperature and derived pressure are recorded state metadata, without thermal exchange. Other aerodynamic models, gusts and particulate interactions are future work. Changed-environment repeats verified native values −8 m/s², 1 kg/m³ and wind [3, 0, −1] m/s; that is an adoption check, not an atmospheric-model calibration.

Preserved failures and corrections
  • The first source build exposed the fork's custom ActorPtr dereference requirement; using GetRef() fixed compilation.
  • The first host startup exposed ASP.NET web-root setup ordering; the root is now passed at builder creation.
  • An early writer design lost 3,251 required records under pressure. That attempt remains Completed / Incomplete / NotReady in the archive. The bounded queue was enlarged and durable writes grouped. Final feature attempts have zero dropped records; broad stress and filesystem-failure qualification remain pending.
  • An early pause assertion read delayed recorder samples. Native event ticks proved the pause; an independent native clock now drives the display and the check.
  • Evidence tooling needed selectors that account for continuous polling and navigation-arrow text. Failed logs remain preserved. A build command used a restore-only flag before correction to the MSBuild RestoreLockedMode property.
